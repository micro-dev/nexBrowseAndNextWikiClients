Article text is fetched from English Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org)

under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 License (CC BY-SA
4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/); additional terms
may apply. The authors of an article are listed in its page history.

Pictures each have their own licence, set by their creator. NextWiki reads
the licence from each picture's file page on Wikipedia and shows only
pictures that are public domain or under a free licence. Pictures whose
licence is not free, or cannot be established, are not shown.

In NextWiki, press A in an article to see its credits: the source, the
article address, where its authors are listed, the text licence and its
terms, the changes NextWiki made, and for each picture its creator,
source, licence, licence terms and any required credit line. The last
page of every article also names Wikipedia as the source and gives the
original article's address.

NextWiki changes the content only to display it on the Spectrum Next: the
text is divided into pages, references, menus and formatting are removed,
characters are simplified to ASCII, and pictures are reduced to 160 x 120
dots in 16 colours.

4. Displaying is not redistributing
-----------------------------------
NextWiki displays Wikipedia content during normal reading only. The
server keeps processed articles and pictures in memory for at most an hour
so that pages can be turned without downloading the article again; nothing
is written to the SD card, and no collection of articles or pictures is
bundled with NextWiki.

If you save, print, publish or otherwise redistribute Wikipedia text or
pictures obtained with NextWiki, you must follow their licences yourself:
credit the authors, name the licence and link to it, indicate changes, and
share adaptations of CC BY-SA material under the same licence. Check each
picture's own licence on its file page; do not assume pictures are free
to reuse.
